How it started

Two grandmothers, one stubborn idea.

Koi Rosso started as two food trucks parked back-to-back — a Tokyo-trained sushi counter and a Roman nonna's sauce pot. Regulars kept ordering from both, then asking why the two never shared a plate. In 2019 they did.

Every roll is cut to order, every sauce still simmers for hours. We just stopped pretending the two kitchens needed separate menus.
