Training

Train with purpose

Four coached programmes and a weekly timetable. Mix them to fit your goal.

01

Strength

Barbell technique, progressive overload and coached lifting in small groups.

  • Small groups, max 8 people
  • Squat, bench, deadlift, press
  • Progress tracked every week

Try it free

02

HIIT

Short, hard intervals with ropes, sleds and bikes. Forty-five minutes, done.

  • 45 minutes, all levels
  • Ropes, sleds, bikes, rowers
  • Heart-rate zones on screen

Try it free

03

Personal training

One coach, one plan, one goal. Sessions built around your body and schedule.

  • 1:1 sessions with your coach
  • Plan adjusted every month
  • Works around injuries

Try it free

04

Mobility & recovery

Stretching, breathing and sauna to keep you training without pain.

  • Stretching and breathing classes
  • Sauna and recovery zone
  • Good for every training day

Try it free
