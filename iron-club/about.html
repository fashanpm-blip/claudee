About

More than a gym

Iron Club is a coached training club. This story is sample content for the concept.

Our story

Coaching first

We started Iron Club for people who wanted more than a membership card: a coach who knows their name, a plan that changes as they get stronger, and a room that makes them want to train.

Every new member starts with a movement check and a written plan.

Facilities

Four zones

Free weights

Racks, platforms and plates up to competition standard.

Machines

Selectorised and plate-loaded machines for every muscle group.

Functional zone

Turf lane, sleds, ropes and rigs for HIIT.

Recovery

Sauna, stretching area and quiet space to cool down.

Values

What we stand for

  1. Progress you can measure

    Numbers, not guesses: lifts, times and check-ins.

  2. Technique before load

    We add weight when the movement is right.

  3. Everyone belongs

    First session or tenth year, you get the same attention.

Your first session is on us

Book a free trial, meet a coach and leave with a plan for your first four weeks.
